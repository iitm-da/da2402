Scatter plot · what r measures

Mechanism Pearson r measures how close the points are to a straight line. Spearman ranks both columns first, so a log axis leaves it at 0.86.

import numpy as np
y7.gdpPercap.corr(y7.lifeExp)
np.log10(y7.gdpPercap).corr(y7.lifeExp)
y7.gdpPercap.corr(y7.lifeExp, method='spearman')
output
0.6787 0.8090 0.8566

Same data, two Pearson values

0.68 on dollars, 0.81 on log dollars. The relation bends; r only scores the straight part.

Labels for r are conventions

The 2025 deck called 0.7 to 1.0 "strong". These cut-offs are habits of a field, not properties of the data. Report r and show the scatter.
